Questions.

How is Walkful different from Apple Health?

Apple Health keeps the numbers. Walkful reads them and puts them in context: what today's steps are linked to in large studies, how your weeks compare, and a guided interval walk when you want a little more. It only reads from Apple Health and never changes anything there.

Do I really need 10,000 steps a day?

No. The round number started as the name of a Japanese pedometer in 1965. A 2025 review of large studies found the lower risk of early death rising most steeply up to around 5,000 to 7,000 steps a day. Walkful suggests 7,000, and you can change it. If you like 10,000, that is still a fine goal.

Does it work with Apple Watch?

Yes, as a source. The steps and active minutes your Apple Watch records reach Apple Health, and Walkful reads them there. There is no Walkful app on the watch yet, so the interval coach runs on your iPhone.

What does it cost?

Walkful is free to download. The free app covers your day, your week, the widgets and the reminders. Walkful Pro is one payment that adds insights, mobility and fitness, records, the interval coach and CSV export. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

Can I share Walkful Pro with my family?

Yes. Walkful Pro has Family Sharing turned on, so one purchase covers everyone in your Family Sharing group. Each of them unlocks Pro on their own iPhone with their own Apple Account, and their steps stay on their own phone, just like yours.

Does Walkful collect or sell my data?

No. Your health data stays on your iPhone. Walkful has no accounts, no servers and no third-party tracking, and its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. This website counts visits without cookies, which is separate from the app.

How do the gentle reminders work?

If you turn them on, iOS lets Walkful look at your recent steps now and then in the background. When you have been still for a while inside the hours you chose, and it has not reminded you lately, you get one gentle notification. iOS decides when those checks run, so a reminder can come late or not at all, and there are at most a couple a day.
