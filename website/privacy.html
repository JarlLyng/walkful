Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary: your health data never leaves your iPhone. Walkful sends anonymous crash reports and two usage counts, with no ID.

The short version

Walkful has no user accounts and no advertising. Your health and activity data is read from Apple Health and processed entirely on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or anyone else. To learn when a release breaks, the app sends anonymous crash and performance reports, and counts how often two things happen, to Sentry. None of it carries an ID, and none of it comes from Apple Health.

Health data

With your explicit permission, Walkful reads activity data from Apple Health (such as steps, walking distance, flights climbed, active minutes and resting heart rate) to show your progress and insights. This data is used only on your device. It is never sent anywhere, never put in a crash report or a count, never shared and never sold. Walkful does not write any data back to Apple Health. You can revoke access at any time in the iOS Health app or Settings.

What the app sends

Crash and performance reports. When the app crashes or freezes, it sends a report to Sentry, a crash reporting service. A report holds the technical details of the fault and coarse facts about the phone: the app version, iOS version, device model and language. Apple's MetricKit diagnostics, such as hangs and disk writes, go the same way.

Two usage counts. The app counts when one of its main screens is opened (Today, Insights, Settings, the interval coach or Walkful Pro) and when you turn on move reminders. A count says only that it happened, with the same coarse facts.

What is never sent: anything from Apple Health, your goal, your settings, your name or anything you type, a screenshot, or any ID. Every install reports as the same anonymous user, so nothing ties a report to you or to your phone, and Walkful keeps nothing on your phone just to measure. Sentry stores no IP addresses for Walkful.

In the App Store this is shown as Diagnostics (crash data, performance data) and Usage Data (product interaction), all not linked to you and not used for tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, such as your daily goal, are stored on your iPhone and nowhere else.

This website

This marketing website (walkful.iamjarl.com) uses Umami, a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics tool, to count anonymous page views. It sets no cookies and does not collect personal data or build profiles.

Children

Walkful collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The anonymous reports above carry no ID and nothing about the person using the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
